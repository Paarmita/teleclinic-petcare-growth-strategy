# PetDoc — 12-Month Retention Roadmap

**Vision:** move PetDoc from one consultation at a time to continuous care for the pet.
**North star:** % of new owners with a second consultation within 180 days, reported per persona. **Today: 21.2%.**
**Year ambition:** ~29% (the optimistic scenario) by the end of quarter 4, with the base case of ~25.9% reached by the end of quarter 2.

> Quarters are counted from the roadmap start (Q1 = months 1–3). Targets are directional, based on 1,200 owners, and every major bet is A/B tested per persona before rollout.

---

## 1. Roadmap at a glance

| | **Q1 — Remember & Follow up** | **Q2 — Plan ahead** | **Q3 — Deepen care** | **Q4 — Everyday companion** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Prove the core loop: PetDoc checks in after the visit and knows the pet when the owner returns | Give new pet parents a reason to come back on a schedule | Make PetDoc the default place for long-term care | Become part of everyday pet life, not just problems |
| **Main persona** | Worried one-timer (Acute, 61%) + all owners | New pet parent (Young animal, 22%) | Care manager (Chronic, 12%) + older pets | All owners, households, existing 50k MAU |
| **Key bets** | Thin pet profile · Vet pre-call summary · **Day-3 check-in** | **Care plan + reminders** · Same-vet re-booking · Scale check-in | Chronic & senior care plans · Refill reminders · Async follow-up (if validated) | Health timeline · Annual wellness · Multi-pet · Recovery tracker |
| **Quick wins** | Care summary · "Vet knew my pet" survey · Service recovery · Book next check · "What's new?" intake | Seasonal nudges · 5★ → save profile · Multi-pet prompt | Auto-attach history to bookings · Milestone content | Personalised content · Win-back for lapsed owners |
| **North-star target** | 21.2% → **~23.5%** | → **~25.9%** | → **~27.5%** | → **~29%** |
| **Lead metric** | Re-check within 14 days 0.7% → 4–5% | Care-plan setup ~50% | Chronic 30-day return 29.5% → 35% | Monthly active pets / owners opening the app without a problem |

## 2. Quarter by quarter

### Q1 — Remember & Follow up (months 1–3)
**Goal:** prove that a follow-up moment plus remembered context brings acute owners back.

| Month | Ship | Idea # |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-tap "the vet already knew my pet" survey (baseline for hidden churn) | B7 |
| 1 | Post-visit care summary: home care, red flags, when to come back | A2 |
| 1 | Service recovery after a failed or frustrating visit | D1 |
| 1–2 | Book your next check before the call ends (chronic) · "Anything new since last time?" intake | B5, B3 |
| 1–3 | **Thin pet profile:** past consultations, uploads, meds saved automatically | B1 |
| 2–3 | **Vet pre-call one-screen summary** (co-designed with Vet Experience) | B2 |
| 2–3 | **Day-3 check-in** "How is Bella today?" → one-tap re-check with the same vet, history attached. A/B test on acute | A1 |
| 3 | Rx delivery tracking + dose reminders (test) | A6 |

**Exit gate (end of Q1):**
- ✅ Scale A1 if check-in response is ≥ 30% and re-checks within 14 days are ≥ 3%, with vets marking < 15% "not needed".
- 🔁 If owners respond but don't book → the need is reassurance → prioritise async follow-up (A4) in Q2/Q3.
- ✅ Re-telling rate trending from 17–19% toward < 10%.

### Q2 — Plan ahead (months 4–6)
**Goal:** turn new pet parents into planned, repeat users and scale the follow-up loop.

| Month | Ship | Idea # |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Scale day-3 check-in to all acute condition types; vet-set dates by condition | A1 |
| 4–5 | **Care plan at first young-animal visit:** vaccines, deworming, check-ups by species/age; reminders + one-tap booking (A/B) | C1 |
| 4–5 | Same-vet re-booking ("Book again with Dr. X") | A3 |
| 5 | Seasonal prevention nudges (ticks, heat, fireworks) | C4 |
| 5–6 | 5★ raters → save pet profile / favourite vet · "Add your other pets" | E1, E2 |
| 6 | Weight / growth tracker for young animals | C3 |

**Exit gate (end of Q2):**
- ✅ Care-plan setup ≥ 40% and reminder → booking ≥ 10%.
- 🔁 If reminders lead to local-clinic visits instead → position PetDoc as the "plan & triage" layer, consider clinic partnerships in Q4.
- ✅ North star at ~25.9% (base case) with no drop in NPS (≥ 65) or rating (≥ 4.8).

### Q3 — Deepen care (months 7–9)
**Goal:** make PetDoc the default for chronic and older pets, and extend to the existing user base.

| Month | Ship | Idea # |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Auto-attach latest results/meds to every booking | B4 |
| 7–8 | Chronic medication refill reminders + one-tap review | B6 |
| 7–9 | **Chronic & senior care plans** (reuse the C1 engine) | C6 |
| 8 | Milestone content: growth, diet, behaviour by age | C2 |
| 8–9 | **Async follow-up test** (48–72h photo/question to the same vet) — only if the Q1 gate showed reassurance demand | A4 |
| 9 | Roll out profile + check-in + care plan to the existing 50k MAU | — |

**Exit gate (end of Q3):**
- ✅ Chronic 30-day return ≥ 33%, re-telling < 5%.
- ✅ Async chat lifts return without cutting paid re-checks or margin (~€10/consult).

### Q4 — Everyday companion (months 10–12)
**Goal:** give owners a reason to open PetDoc when nothing is wrong.

| Month | Ship | Idea # |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Annual wellness reminder for one-time acute owners | C5 |
| 10–11 | Pet health timeline: one view of every episode, plan, weight and med | B1 v2 |
| 10–11 | Recovery tracker (daily photo/symptom log shared with the vet) | A5 |
| 11 | Win-back for lapsed owners using pet profile data (e.g. "Bella's booster is due") | — |
| 11–12 | Household / multi-pet experience | E2 v2 |
| 12 | Explore partnerships (pharmacy, local clinics) with Growth and Ops | D4 |

**Exit gate (end of Q4):**
- ✅ North star ~29%; 6-month contribution per new owner up ~8% (€11.80 → €12.70+).
- ✅ Share of owners opening the app without a consultation growing month on month.

## 3. Always-on tracks (all year)

| Track | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Friction (advocate)** | Pre-call connection check + audio fallback (D2), reliable uploads (D3), Rx delivery ≤ 4 days (D4). Worth ~+3pp combined | Other missions; we track and push |
| **Measurement** | North star per persona, leading metrics, the "vet knew my pet" score, monthly cohort review | Retention PM + Data |
| **Experimentation** | A/B test each bet per persona; holdout group kept all year to measure the combined effect | Retention PM + Data |
| **Vet experience** | Summary and check-in fit the vet's workflow; consultation length and vet satisfaction stable | Vet Experience mission |
| **Guardrails** | Rating ≥ 4.8, NPS ≥ 65, notification opt-outs, re-checks marked "not needed", margin ~€10/consult | All |

---

## 4. Dependencies

- **Thin pet profile (Q1)** unblocks the vet summary, check-in history, same-vet re-booking, care plans and the health timeline. It is the critical path.
- **Care plan engine (Q2)** is reused for chronic and senior plans (Q3) and win-back (Q4).
- **Vet Experience** sign-off is needed before the vet summary and check-in ship.
- **Growth:** agree who owns the care-plan sign-up flow (Q2) and partnership work (Q4).

## 6. Risks and how the roadmap adapts

| Risk | Signal | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Owners want reassurance, not a paid re-check | Check-in responses high, bookings low | Bring async follow-up (A4) forward to Q2 |
| Care-plan reminders send owners to local clinics | Plans set up, low PetDoc bookings | Reposition as triage + plan; explore clinic partnerships |
| Re-telling falls but return doesn't move | Survey up, return flat | Look deeper at vet quality and consistency |
| Notification fatigue | Opt-outs rising faster than returns | Cap frequency, make check-ins opt-in by condition |
| Small samples (chronic 149 owners) | Wide confidence intervals | Longer tests, pool across quarters |
