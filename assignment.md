Case Study — Senior Product Manager, PatientExperience (m/w/d)

## The company
PetDoc is Germany's leading pet telemedicine app. Vets by video and chat, from home,when you need them.

## What PetDoc does
- Video consultations with vets (scheduled or on-demand)
- Chat with vets (share photos and documents)
- Prescriptions and medication, issued after a video consultation and shipped via a partner online pharmacy

## Business model
Pay-per-visit, €35 to €45 per consultation. After vet fees, payment processing and infrastructure, the contribution margin is roughly €10 per consultation.
Current numbers
- 50,000 MAU
- ~2,800 new registrations per month
- 120 vets on the platform
- 4.8 stars in the App Store, NPS 65
- Only 23% of users come back for a second consultation within 180 days

## The situation
You're the Senior Product Manager for Patient Experience at PetDoc. The first visit goes well. Users rate it 4.8 stars and the NPS is 65. The second visit often doesn't happen.

PetDoc works in missions. Each has its own goals, its own team and its own roadmap.
- Patient Experience, your mission. The pet owner's experience from the moment they're here: the journey around the consultation, everything that happens afterwards, and the question of why someone comes back.
- Vet Experience, everything vets do on the platform.
- Growth, acquisition, activation through new use cases,opening up new user groups.

Pricing and business model are fixed for this exercise. If your concept touches another mission, and good concepts often do, describe what you need from them and how you'd get it.
Your mission's goal for this quarter is set: more users come back to PetDoc for a second consultation. Which problem you solve to get there, and what your team builds, is your call.

## The data
Attached is a CSV covering a representative sample of consultations from a six-month  window, 1 August 2025 to 31 January 2026: 1,413 consultations from 1,200 pet owners. One row per consultation. The data was extracted on 1 August 2026, so every consultation has had a full 180 days to produce a follow-up.

## Your task

#### Deliverable 1: Feature concept
The concept answers:
1. Which problem, for which users, with quantified impact potential. Concrete enough that stakeholders understand why you picked this problem space and solution and not another option you considered.
2. The feature. Described or visualised concretely enough that design and engineering can build on it.
3. Who you'd involve, inside your mission or in others, before the concept goes into implementation.
4. How you'd validate your hypothesis, and what would tell you that you were wrong.

#### Deliverable 2: Working prototype
Use the AI tools of your choice (Lovable, Claude, v0, Figma Make, whatever) to build a clickable prototype of your concept. Not the whole app. Just the point in the flow where it's decided whether your
hypothesis holds.

#### Deliverable 3: Screen recording, max 5 minutes
Walk us through your decisions: what you discarded, what you chose, and why. We’re looking for an unedited screen recording with a voiceover (Loom, QuickTime, any free tool works). We're assessing the content, not the production. No editing, no slides needed.
