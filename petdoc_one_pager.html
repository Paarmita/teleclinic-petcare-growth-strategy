2Problem & strategy

PetDoc treats each consultation as a one-off transaction. Nothing pulls owners back after the visit, and nothing remembers them when they return.

Remember · foundation

PetDoc knows your pet

Thin pet profile, one-screen vet summary before the call, “anything new?” intake.

The visit doesn't end with the call

Deliberately left out: video/audio, uploads and Rx delivery (other missions; ~+3–4pp combined, we track and push). Pricing is fixed. Wait time matters only above 15 min.
