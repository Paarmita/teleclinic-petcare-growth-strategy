Your tasks
Four million treatments, 4.9 stars: TeleClinic is Germany's leading platform for online doctor visits. Our ambition: a loved brand where every touchpoint is a 5-star experience – and which becomes an indispensable, everyday companion for patients' health. An app they open not just once, but again and again: daily, weekly, monthly, for a wide range of health concerns.
As Senior Product Manager – Patient Experience (m/f/d), you will shape this across iOS, Android, and web, with engagement and retention as your North Star metrics – and thus contribute to making TeleClinic one of the most widely used health apps in Germany.

Your role

Strategy & Roadmap Ownership: The goals of the Patient Experience Mission provide the direction; you design the roadmap. You identify the levers that drive the 5-star patient experience and iteratively develop our goals further.

End-to-End Discovery: You identify real patient problems in feedback, data, and the market, quickly develop ideas, and assess their impact. In close collaboration with your own and other teams, this becomes a prioritized concept: refined, agreed upon, (AI) prototyped, ready for implementation, and entirely under your control.

Experiments & Iteration: Before you develop a feature, you test the hypothesis. You design experiments end-to-end, define success criteria, and validate what works and what doesn't through user feedback and data. After the experiment, you decide whether to roll out or roll back the feature.

Collaboration: You will work closely with Patient Growth, Patient Partnerships, Doctor Experience and other missions to create outstanding end-to-end patient journeys.

Team: You'll manage your mission's roadmap as part of a trio with an Engineering Manager and a Product Designer, supported by nine Engineers including QA. iOS, Android, Web, Low Code: Nothing in our stack limits what you can offer patients.


Your profile

Product Craft: Several years of experience as a Product Manager for consumer products, ideally with a focus on Product Experience and Engagement.

Discovery : Experience in clarifying the direction in unclear problem areas, aligning teams with a clear strategy, and translating this into product solutions.

Product awareness: You recognize when a solution is good enough and when it isn't — and can explain why. A strong aesthetic sense and an eye for detail that delights patients are a clear advantage.

Platform: Experience with native iOS, Android, and React applications. Good understanding of the technical requirements and limitations of these platforms.

Communication & Documentation: In a remote-first work environment, you rely on excellent written communication and documentation to unite stakeholders behind your vision.

Data: You have several years of experience using analysis tools such as Metabase, Mixpanel or similar to estimate potential, quantify user behavior and evaluate experiments.

Process & Team: You have experience in actively shaping the discovery and delivery processes of a product team and continuously developing its effectiveness and efficiency.

Language: Our working language is English, our patients write in German. You are fluent in both.

What happens after you apply?
1. Getting to know each other:
Once we receive your application, our recruiting team will review your profile. If it's a good fit, we'll invite you for an initial interview where we can exchange further information and discuss the process and next steps.
2 Interview:
Get to know your potential future leader! This conversation is about finding out if we are a good fit professionally, personally, and culturally.
3 Practical task:
Now things get concrete! Depending on the role, you'll receive a test task, a coding challenge, or a business case. This allows us to see how you approach challenges and apply your skills in practice. In this step, we'll go into detail about the job's technical requirements.
4. Team meeting:
An interview with the team will take place, in which you will discuss the test project you have worked on and get to know each other better.
5 Management Interview:
In the final interview with C-level executives, you can expect a discussion focusing on mindset and cultural fit, and you'll gain insight into TeleClinic's strategic direction. Especially given our 100% remote work model, it's important to us that you and we have all the necessary information to make an informed decision – for a perfect match at TeleClinic
